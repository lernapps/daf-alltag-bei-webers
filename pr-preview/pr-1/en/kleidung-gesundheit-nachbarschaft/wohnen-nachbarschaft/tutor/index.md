## Context: the page “Housing and Neighborhood Norms”

Explain everyday housing norms in Germany such as Hausordnung, quiet hours, ventilation, and waste separation.

### House Rules and Waste Separation at the Webers'

Peter quickly learns three typical rules of everyday housing in Germany: **Ruhezeiten** (quiet hours from 10 p.m. and all day Sunday — no lawnmowing or drilling!), **Stoßlüften** (opening windows wide briefly rather than leaving them tilted), and **Mülltrennung** (waste separation).

Waste separation in Lindenberg (typical across Germany):
- Bin / Container | What goes in?
- Yellow bin / yellow bag | Plastic, metal, and composite packaging (e.g. yogurt cups, cans).
- Blue bin (paper) | Newspapers, cardboard boxes, clean paper.
- Brown / green bin (organic) | Fruit and vegetable peels, coffee grounds, food scraps.
- Black / gray bin (residual) | Non-recyclable waste (e.g. used tissues, vacuum bags).
- Glass containers (street drop-off) | Non-deposit glass bottles — sorted by color (clear, brown, green) and dropped off only during daytime on weekdays/Saturdays!

**Remember: Hausordnung in Apartment Buildings**

In apartment buildings, the **Hausordnung** (house rules) often hangs in the stairwell: it sets quiet hours (typically 10 p.m.–6 a.m. and Sundays), stairwell cleaning duties, and where bicycles may be parked.

### Exercise ruhezeiten-regeln

- Task: It is Sunday at 2:30 p.m. in Lindenberg. Which activities violate typical German Sunday quiet hours?
- Answer format: choose all right answers
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ Mowing the lawn or drilling holes into the wall. – Correct! Loud gardening and DIY work are prohibited on Sundays in Germany.
  - ✓ Throwing empty glass bottles into the street glass recycling container. – True! Glass containers post drop-off hours (usually Mon–Sat 7 a.m.–8 p.m.); Sundays are prohibited due to noise.
  - ✗ Enjoying coffee and cake in the living room with the host family. – Normal conversation at indoor volume is of course always allowed.
- Solution: Mowing the lawn or drilling holes into the wall.; Throwing empty glass bottles into the street glass recycling container.
- Explanation: German Sunday quiet hours protect neighbors from loud noise like lawnmowing, drilling, and glass-container drop-offs.
