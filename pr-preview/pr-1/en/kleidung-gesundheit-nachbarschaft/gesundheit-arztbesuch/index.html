At the Family Doctor's and the Pharmacy

Peter's cold gets worse: he has a Husten (cough), Schnupfen (runny nose), and a slight Fieber (fever). Frau Weber calls the Arztpraxis (doctor's office) to book a Termin (appointment). Peter brings his Versichertenkarte (health insurance card), then picks up his medication with a Rezept (prescription) at the Apotheke (pharmacy).

Understanding the Doctor's Advice

  • „Nehmen Sie die Tabletten dreimal täglich nach dem Essen.“ (Take the tablets three times a day after meals.)
  • „Bleiben Sie drei Tage im Bett und trinken Sie viel Tee.“ (Stay in bed for three days and drink lots of tea.)
  • „Hier ist Ihre Krankschreibung für die Schule.“ (Here is your sick note for school.)

Exercise

Peter's medication label says: „Zweimal täglich eine Tablette vor dem Essen.“ What should Peter do?

  • He takes one tablet twice a day, before eating. right answer

    Exactly: 'zweimal täglich' = twice a day, 'vor dem Essen' = before meals.

  • He takes two tablets at once after dinner.

    'zweimal täglich eine Tablette' means one tablet at two different times of day, and 'vor' means before.

  • He takes only one tablet per week.

    'täglich' means daily, not weekly.

Key dosage phrases are 'einmal / zweimal / dreimal täglich' (1x/2x/3x daily) and 'vor / nach dem Essen' (before / after meals).
