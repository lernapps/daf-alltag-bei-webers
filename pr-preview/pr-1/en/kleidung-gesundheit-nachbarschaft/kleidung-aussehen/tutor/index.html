Context: the page “Clothing and Appearance”

Describe clothes, sizes, physical appearance, and basic personality traits in German.

Inside the Weather-and-Wardrobe Closet

Winter in Lindenberg is getting colder, so Peter goes shopping with Lukas and updates the wardrobe closet on his board. Three A2 phrases help when trying things on: Welche Größe haben Sie? (What size do you wear?), Die Jacke passt gut (fits), and Blau steht dir! (suits you!).

Vocabulary (German – English): Clothing, appearance, and personality

  • die Jacke / der Mantel, "die Jacken / die Mäntel" – jacket / coat
  • der Pullover / das T-Shirt – sweater / T-shirt
  • die Hose / das Kleid / der Rock – trousers / dress / skirt
  • die Schuhe / die Stiefel – shoes / boots (plural)
  • die Größe (S, M, L / 38, 42) – clothing or shoe size. Example: Haben Sie den Pullover in Größe M? – Do you have the sweater in size M?
  • passen / stehen – to fit (size) / to suit (look). Note: 'passen' is about size/fit; 'stehen' is about how something looks on someone.
  • groß / klein / schlank – tall / short / slim
  • blonde / braune / lange / kurze Haare – blond / brown / long / short hair
  • freundlich / hilfsbereit / lustig / ruhig – friendly / helpful / funny / quiet. Example: Nadia ist sehr hilfsbereit und trägt eine Brille. – Nadia is very helpful and wears glasses.

Exercise passen-oder-stehen

  • Task: Peter tries on a warm blue jacket. Size M is just right, neither too tight nor too loose. What does Lukas say?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Die Jacke passt dir super! – Correct! For the right size/fit, German uses 'passen'.
    • ✗ Die Jacke ist zu klein für dich. – It is just right, so not too small.
    • ✗ Die Jacke wohnt dir gut. – 'wohnen' means 'to live (in a place)' and is never used for clothes.
  • Solution: Die Jacke passt dir super!
  • Explanation: 'passen' describes the right size/fit; 'stehen' describes whether a color or style looks good on someone.

Exercise kleidung-beschreibung

  • Task: Complete Peter's description of his winter outfit and of Nadia.
  • Answer format: fill in the gaps in German
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters wrong, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. In winter Peter wears a warm jacket. → Im Winter trägt Peter eine warme Jacke.
  2. Do you have the sweater in size M? → Haben Sie den Pullover in Größe M?
  3. Nadia wears glasses and is very helpful. → Nadia trägt eine Brille und ist sehr hilfsbereit.
  • The app's hint: Capitalize nouns (Jacke, Größe, Brille) and keep the adjective 'hilfsbereit' lowercase.
  • Explanation: Combining clothing items, sizes, glasses, and personality adjectives lets you describe people clearly.
  • How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words. Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.
