Sam Catches a Cold

In autumn, Sam catches a cold in Lindenberg. On the health check of his board, he writes down key body parts and two patterns to say something hurts: Mein Kopf tut weh (singular) / Meine Ohren tun weh (plural), or Ich habe Kopfschmerzen (I have a headache).

Match the German body parts to their English meanings.

Right answer: der Hals – throat / neck; der Bauch – stomach; der Rücken – back; das Bein – leg

With these body parts plus '-schmerzen', you can name almost any ache: Halsschmerzen, Bauchschmerzen, Rückenschmerzen.

Exercise

Choose the right word for Sam's conversation with Frau Weber.

  1. My head hurts. → Mein Kopf … weh.
  2. My legs hurt after soccer. → Meine Beine … weh.
  3. I am sick today. → Ich bin heute ….
  4. Frau Weber says: Get well soon! → Frau Weber sagt: Gute …!
Solution
  1. Mein Kopf tut weh.
  2. Meine Beine tun weh.
  3. Ich bin heute krank.
  4. Frau Weber sagt: Gute Besserung!

One body part (singular) takes 'tut weh'; multiple body parts (plural) take 'tun weh'. When someone is sick, you wish them 'Gute Besserung!'.
