The Webers' Holiday Calendar

A large yearly calendar hangs in the Webers' kitchen. On public holidays (Feiertage), schools and almost all shops in Germany are closed. Sam marks the major holidays and their greetings.

Major public holidays and traditions in Germany
Holiday & TimeTraditions & Greeting
New Year's Day (Jan 1)After Silvester (Dec 31, midnight fireworks), people say: „Frohes neues Jahr!“
Easter (spring)Painting and hunting colored Easter eggs, Easter bunny. Greeting: „Frohe Ostern!“
German Unity Day (Oct 3)Germany's national day (reunification in 1990); shops are closed.
Christmas (Dec 24–26)Advent calendar, Christmas market, gifts on Christmas Eve (Dec 24). Greeting: „Frohe Weihnachten!“

Good to Know: Heiligabend and Public Holidays

In Germany, families open Christmas presents on the evening of December 24 (Heiligabend). December 25 and 26 are official public holidays. Before New Year's Eve, people wish each other „Guten Rutsch!“ (Have a good slide into the new year!).
