Quick Refresher

For the everyday dialogues in this chapter, Peter draws on two building blocks from his arrival in Lindenberg:

  • Understanding short announcements: I can pick out key details like times, prices, and platform numbers from short announcements and voicemails.
  • W-questions: I can ask and answer W-questions using wer, was, wo, woher, wohin, wann, and wie (Wann öffnet die Apotheke? Wie teuer ist der Tee?).

Exercise

Peter wants to know what time the bakery opens on Saturday. Which W-question does he ask?

  • Wann öffnet die Bäckerei am Samstag? right answer

    Correct! You ask about a time or point in time using 'Wann'.

  • Woher öffnet die Bäckerei am Samstag?

    'Woher' asks about origin (Where from?), not time.

  • Wer öffnet die Bäckerei am Samstag?

    'Wer' asks about a person (Who?), not opening hours.

In W-questions, the question word sits in position 1 and the conjugated verb in position 2: Wann öffnet …?
